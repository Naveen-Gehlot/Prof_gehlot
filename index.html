About

From muscle signals to machines that explain themselves

I joined the School of Electrical Engineering at Manipal Institute of Technology in July 2025 as an Assistant Professor. My Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering at MNIT Jaipur, completed in 2026, focused on surface electromyography (sEMG): recognising hand gestures from muscle activity and using them to drive robotic hands and prosthetic devices.

A recurring question in my work is why a model made a given decision. I use explainable AI to choose features and check models, and spiking neural networks to cut the energy cost of real-time control on small devices. In 2025 I spent three months as a visiting research fellow at Western Norway University of Applied Sciences in Bergen.

Research

Five lines of work

Explainable biosignal analysis

Fusing explainability methods to select sEMG features for hand gesture recognition, with XAI also applied to EEG limb-task identification and clinical risk prediction.

Hand gesture recognition

1D convolutional architecture search, L-SHADE optimised learning and a mixture-of-experts Extra Trees model for recognising gestures from sEMG.

Spiking neural networks

Energy-efficient frameworks for real-time robotic arm manipulation, plasticity-based learning, neurotransmitter modulation and image classification.

Robotics and prosthetics

Controlling a robotic hand from sEMG gestures, a wearable sEMG prosthesis, a 3-DOF robotic arm and a hand anthropometry survey for ergonomic and bionic design.

Healthcare AI

COVID-19 and paediatric chest X-ray classification, knee abnormality analysis from sEMG, chronic kidney disease risk and musculoskeletal disorders in garment workers.

Collaborations

Neural forecasting for wind and solar power, deep reinforcement learning for autonomous driving and neural network models for concrete strength testing.
